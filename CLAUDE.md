# PestiAlvilag code map

A 1932 Budapest GTA 3 style game. Unreal 5.8.4, C++ only: almost everything is built in code at runtime, the levels are nearly empty.
Read this first. Open only the files your task needs, and grep big files instead of reading them whole.

## Where things start
- `Source/PestiAlvilag/PestiAlvilagGameModeBase.cpp` spawns every system (world, traffic, people, missions, landmarks). Start here to find who owns what. `PestiStart` holds start spots and yaws.
- Map data comes from `Tools/OsmGraybox/` (Python): `osm_to_graybox.py` (streets, blocks), `street_furniture.py` (lamps, benches, pavements, shops, walker lines), `trams_1932.py` (tram lines), `buses_1932.py` (bus lines), `city_details.py` (open courtyards, Corvin, wall adverts, kiosks, clocks, map icons). Output JSON lives under `Data/` and is read by `OsmGraybox.cpp`, `StreetFurniture.cpp`, `Pedestrians.cpp`, `Knockables.cpp`.

## Naming pattern
- `*Model.h` (namespace, header only): builds the boxes/shapes of one thing (CarModel, PersonKit, TramModel, ShopModel, MarketHallModel...). Change looks here.
- `*Synth.h` + `*Sound.cpp`: sounds made in code (EngineSound, TramSound, HorseSound, FightSound).
- The matching `.cpp/.h` actor holds behaviour.

## Areas (files in Source/PestiAlvilag)
- Player on foot: `PestiPerson` (big, ~1400 lines: moves, camera, F/T keys), `FreeRun` (vault/slide), `Brawl.h` (fist fighting), `FightAnim.h` (fight animation layer: moves melt into each other, hit springs, breathing, planted feet, key-pose helper for new moves; Space sidestep lives in Brawl/PestiPerson).
- Player car: `PestiCar`, `CarBody`, `CarVisuals`, `CarModel.h` (all car bodies, also Dr. Halász's Steyr for mission 7), `ExhaustSmoke`, `EngineSound`, `Wreck.h`, `CarDamage.h` (crash dents, broken lamps and glass, parts falling off, wear; used by CarVisuals::Knock). Crash effects: `CrashBits` (sparks, glass and grit bits, dust, one mesh like ExhaustSmoke) + `CrashSound`/`CrashSynth.h` (bump, crunch, glass, clang, wall scrape made in code), started from APestiCar::CrashFeedback.
- Traffic and police: `TrafficManager`, `TrafficCar`, `PoliceCar`, `PoliceModel.h`, `PolicePatrols` (constables, phone boxes), `CarjackedDriver`.
- Taxis: `PestiTaxi`, `TaxiCompany` (job), `TaxiRides` (ride as passenger), `TaxiPassenger`.
- Trams: `Tram`, `TramModel.h`, `TramRides`, `TramSound`/`TramSynth.h`.
- City sounds: `SoundSpots` (sound spots placed about the map: church tower, Keleti, markets, pigeons, park, café, courtyards; plays the nearest few) + `SpotSound`/`CitySynth.h` (each kind of spot's sound made in code; later can be swapped for recordings).
- Horse cart: `HorseCart`, `HorseCartModel.h`, `HorseSound`/`HorseSynth.h`; `StableYard` + `StableYardModel.h` (carters' yard on Auróra utca where the cart waits, Jóska the stable lad).
- Bicycle: `BicycleModel.h` (kinds: shop bike with small front wheel, roadster with a basket on the fork, Csepel ladies' bike, plain gents' bike; enamel colours; rider pose on the pedals, sprint pose) + `Bicycle` (ABicycle: ride it, F on/off, Shift sprint, B bell; one parked by Bözsi néni's door, both kinds in the test grounds) + `BikeBellSound` (bell made in code) + `TrafficBike` (ATrafficBike: city riders along the kerb, spawned with parked bikes by `TrafficManager`; wobble/fall when bumped, F pulls the rider off).
- Bus: `BusModel.h` (MÁVAG Harcsa look) + `Bus` (ABus: a tram on a bus route, lines 12, 20, 5 from `buses_1932.py`; riding via `TramRides`).
- People: `PersonKit.h` + `PersonModel` (bodies, clothes), `Pedestrians` + `WalkingPerson` (walkers), `StoryPerson` (named characters), `ParkKeeper`, `Pigeons`/`PigeonModel.h`, `Animals` + `AnimalModel.h` (cats and dogs: Lafi and Bütyök in Laci's yard, strays in yards and squares; flee, bark, leap clear of cars, never hurt) + `AnimalSound`/`AnimalSynth.h` (bark, growl, meow, hiss), `ChaseThief`/`ChaseModel.h`, `Ruffian` (mission 4 gang, group fight).
- World and look: `OsmGraybox` (streets, buildings, facades), `StreetFurniture` (big: lamps, benches, shops), `ShopModel.h`, `GroundSurfaces` + `SurfaceModel.h` (cobbles, kerbs, market earth), `DayNight` (sun, lamps, glass at night, cloud and fog via SetWeather), `FallingLeaves`, `ParkModel.h`, `Knockables`/`KnockModel.h`.
- Bandi's boxing yard: `BoxingYard` (ABoxingYard: grund on Kis Fuvaros utca, open after mission 3; gold ring in the ring starts the next of 5 fights, ARuffian Box... kinds; E by Bandi opens his lessons menu) + `BoxingYardModel.h` (fence, fire walls, chalk ring, crowd spots, Bandi and rivals' clothes). Fight points (bunyópont) and lessons live in the game mode (AddFightPoints, KnowsLesson), the 4 learnt moves in Brawl.h (BodyBlow, SlipCounter, Headbutt, WallThrow) and PestiPerson (BellyPunch, SlipPunch, ButtHim, ThrowAtWall). Laci's garage and the yard show on the maps (APestiHUD::MarkPlace).
- Weather and smoke: `Weather` (AWeather: spells of clear, cloud, rain, fog; rain streaks round the camera; Esc > PRÓBATEREP row IDŐJÁRÁS forces one) + `RainSound`/`RainSynth.h`; `ChimneySmoke` (brick stacks put on flat roofs round the camera, coal smoke, steam of the two engines at Keleti via AKeletiStation::SteamSpots).
- Landmarks: `Nepszinhaz`, `KeletiStation` (big), `KeletiEagle`/`EagleModel.h`, `MarketHall` + `MarketHallModel.h` + `HallMarketModel.h`, `FleaMarket` (biggest, ~1600 lines: Teleki and hall markets) + `MarketModel.h`, `Cafe`, `FoodVendor`, `FoodMenus`, `HomeDoor`.
- Missions and saving: `Mission` (base; a mission can wait in a gold start ring, `StartRingComponent`, and steps can play short scenes, `Cutscene`: black bars, camera shots, the mission's title, Space skips), `MissionPrologue`, `MissionUzenet`, `MissionZsebtolvaj`, `MissionZsibvasar`, `MissionFelmondas` (mission 5, from a real news story) + `GarayYard`/`GarayModel.h` (Garay utca 13: Hermann's shop, Schwimmer's timber yard, their people; after mission 5 AGarayYard puts old Hermann back in the shop, bandaged via PersonKit bBandaged, with his clerk), `MissionKifutofiu` (mission 6: errand boy, 3 parcels by shop bicycle against the clock) + `DeliveryModel.h` (its shop, delivery scenes and people), `MissionElsoAuto` (mission 7: steal Dr. Halász's Steyr on Kiss József utca past a constable, lose the police, bring it to Laci) + `LaciYard`/`LaciYardModel.h` (Laci's motor workshop yard on Szerdahelyi utca, Laci the mechanic; after mission 7 also the garage: stop a car over the pit to get it mended and repainted for 3 P, which loses the police), `MissionBunda` (mission 8, end of act 1: fur coat from Friedmann's furrier on Rákóczi út (`FurrierModel.h`: shop, coat, phone kiosk, the inspector's wife), night raid on Bözsi's house, escape over the courtyard balconies, newspaper (with a side column of short news about earlier deeds, APestiHUD::SetClippingBrief), phone call, home moves to an attic on Bérkocsis utca) + `BozsiHouseModel.h` (Bözsi's tenement Népszínház u. 29 with courtyard, balconies, stair, wash-house, back yard; replaces its map block, OsmGraybox leaves it out), `PestiSaveGame`, `Lighters`/`LighterModel.h` (collectibles), `GoalMarkerComponent`, `RouteGuideComponent`.
- City details: `CityDetails` (ACityDetails: Corvin department store front on Blaha, 6 open gateways + courtyards with gangs, painted fire-wall adverts, newspaper kiosks, street clocks whose hands follow ADayNight, sends the maps' place icons via APestiHUD::SetMapIcons) + `CityDetailsModel.h` (their shapes). Data from `Tools/OsmGraybox/city_details.py` -> `Data/OsmGraybox/city_details.json`; OsmGraybox swaps in its cut/raised blocks, StreetFurniture leaves those gates open (ShopModel::BuildGate bOpen).
- Shape sets: `ShapeSet` (AShapeSet: draws a list of MarketModel shapes, lettering and small night lamps; used for Bözsi's house, the furrier, the kiosk, the carried coat).
- Screen text: `PestiHud` (art deco HUD, menus, minimap and the M big map with your own mark), `PestiMap` (bakes the city plan picture and street names for the maps), `PestiText.h` (Hungarian/English strings).
- Test grounds: `TestGrounds` + `TestGroundsModel.h` (far west of the city, built on first use from the Esc menu row PRÓBATEREP: fight yard with number keys for the thief/ruffians/shop people, vehicle yard with every car, the horse cart, a wall, wrecks and a track).
- Menus: `PestiMenu` (title screen "Pesti Mesék", Esc/Tab pause menu, settings, controls, credits; logic and keys, plus PestiSettings: volume, mouse, English subtitles). Painted by `PestiHud` The PRÓBATEREP page also has KÜLDETÉSEK, a list of the 8 act 1 missions: picking one calls `GameMode::JumpToMission` (level restarts with earlier missions done, save on disk untouched, player put by the mission's ring via `AMission::GetStartSpot`).

## Rules for changes
- Claude can't build Unreal; Daniel does, and each mistake costs him a failed build. Check carefully before pushing.
- Known build traps (more in project memory unreal-build-gotchas): include the full header of any component type you convert or call; never name locals after AActor members (Children, Tags, Owner...) or after names in visible namespaces (Brawl, FreeRun); FString::Printf format must be one plain TEXT literal; use \uXXXX escapes for Hungarian letters inside TEXT("...").
- Never push binaries (.uasset, .umap, most .png): Git LFS is blocked from the cloud.
- When you add a new file, add one line for it in this map.
