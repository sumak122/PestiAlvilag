# PestiAlvilag code map

A 1932 Budapest GTA 3 style game. Unreal 5.8.4, C++ only: almost everything is built in code at runtime, the levels are nearly empty.
Read this first. Open only the files your task needs, and grep big files instead of reading them whole.

## Where things start
- `Source/PestiAlvilag/PestiAlvilagGameModeBase.cpp` spawns every system (world, traffic, people, missions, landmarks). Start here to find who owns what. `PestiStart` holds start spots and yaws.
- Map data comes from `Tools/OsmGraybox/` (Python): `osm_to_graybox.py` (streets, blocks), `street_furniture.py` (lamps, benches, pavements, shops, walker lines), `trams_1932.py` (tram lines), `buses_1932.py` (bus lines), `city_details.py` (open courtyards, Corvin, wall adverts, kiosks, clocks, map icons). Output JSON lives under `Data/` and is read by `OsmGraybox.cpp`, `StreetFurniture.cpp`, `Pedestrians.cpp`, `Knockables.cpp`.

## Naming pattern
- `*Model.h` (namespace, header only): builds the boxes/shapes of one thing (CarModel, PersonKit, TramModel, ShopModel, MarketHallModel...). Change looks here.
- `*Synth.h` + `*Sound.cpp`: sounds made in code (EngineSound, TramSound, HorseSound, FightSound).
- The matching `.cpp/.h` actor holds behaviour.

## Areas (files in Source/PestiAlvilag)
- Player on foot: `PestiPerson` (big, ~1400 lines: moves, camera, F/T keys), `WalkAnim.h` (FWalker: walk/run/sprint strides, body bob, lean, bank into turns, idle breathing and weight shift, jump push-off/tuck/landing dip; tells when a foot lands; used by APestiPerson::SwingLimbs), `FootSound` + `FootSynth.h` (the player's quiet footsteps made in code, by ground: cobbles, slabs, gravel, grass, earth, and wet; ground found via GroundSurfaces::KindOf in APestiPerson::PlayFootstep), `FreeRun` (vault/slide), `Brawl.h` (fist fighting), `FightAnim.h` (fight animation layer: moves melt into each other, hit springs, breathing, planted feet, key-pose helper for new moves; Space sidestep lives in Brawl/PestiPerson).
- Player car: `PestiCar`, `CarBody`, `CarVisuals`, `CarModel.h` (all car bodies, also Dr. Halász's Steyr for mission 7), `ExhaustSmoke`, `EngineSound`, `Wreck.h`, `CarDamage.h` (crash dents, broken lamps and glass, parts falling off: lamps, bumpers, doors, bonnet with the engine bay under it, and wheels in fast side crashes (CarVisuals::DropWheel, the car sags); wear; used by CarVisuals::Knock). Old cars (CarModel::IsOld: Ford T, town car) start with the crank (E) and stall (APestiCar). Locked cars (APestiCar::SetLocked: Laci's ordered cars, two in the test yard): tap F to smash the window (loud), hold F to pick the lock (APestiPerson::TickBreakIn). Crash effects: `CrashBits` (sparks, glass and grit bits, dust, one mesh like ExhaustSmoke) + `CrashSound`/`CrashSynth.h` (bump, crunch, glass, clang, wall scrape made in code), started from APestiCar::CrashFeedback.
  Handling and crash physics: `CarHandling.h` (each EBody's weight, top speed, pull, brakes, steering, grip, gear change time as shares of the saloon; Collide: momentum and spin trade between two cars or a car and a wall). APestiCar: weight shift (braking tucks in, pulling runs wide), wet setts grip/brake less (AWeather::GetWet), crash spin (SpinRate), parked cars slide when shoved (Shove, RollDriverless), tyre squeal/hiss (CrashSynth skid, UCrashSound::SetSkid), wider view with speed. ATrafficCar::TakeHit: knocked traffic and police cars slide and spin (TickKnocked), knock others, then rejoin a lane.
- Traffic and police: `TrafficManager`, `TrafficCar`, `PoliceCar` (chases; near the player it pulls up and two constables get out to arrest him; faster and from further at more stars, rams only at 3; out of sight drives to where he was last seen and searches round it), `PoliceModel.h`, `PolicePatrols` (constables, phone boxes; APoliceBox::Knock/Smash: 3 blows on foot or a car hit smash the glass (PoliceModel::BuildBroken: jagged bits and shards), a car hit tips it, nobody phones from a smashed box, mended later out of sight), `CarjackedDriver`, `Witnesses` (AWitnesses: a crime the police did not see (sites calling APolicePatrols::Witnessed call AWitnesses::Report) now and then, rarer with better hírnév, sends a passer-by (APedestrians::TakeWitness) running to the nearest working box with a red ! over him and on the minimap; he phones (APedestrian::PhoneAt) and the player is wanted; a click fights him, E pays him 1 P; a smashed box: he shakes his fist (APedestrian::ShakeFist) and goes).
  Wanted rules live in the game mode (UpdateWanted: search circle GetLastSeenAt/GetSearchRadius drawn on the maps by PestiHud PaintSearch, hiding in courtyard gateways via ACityDetails::IsHidingPlace, Busted: fine, hírnév, car kept, walks out of the station). `PoliceChase` (APoliceChase: the VIII. district police station door on Víg utca with guards, road blocks at 3 stars: barriers knocked flying by the car, traffic stops at BlockPoints) + `PoliceStationModel.h` (station front, road barrier). Crashing into traffic gives a star only if police see it (PestiCar). Walkers wait at the kerb on crossings while a car comes (Pedestrians, street_furniture.json "crossings").

## Rules for changes
- Claude can't build Unreal; Daniel does, and each mistake costs him a failed build. Check carefully before pushing.
- Known build traps (more in project memory unreal-build-gotchas): include the full header of any component type you convert or call; never name locals after AActor members (Children, Tags, Owner...) or after names in visible namespaces (Brawl, FreeRun); FString::Printf format must be one plain TEXT literal; use \uXXXX escapes for Hungarian letters inside TEXT("...").
- Never push binaries (.uasset, .umap, most .png): Git LFS is blocked from the cloud.
- When you add a new file, add one line for it in this map.
